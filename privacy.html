Privacy on the waiting list

This covers areteprograms.net, the coming soon page for Arete, and its waiting list. The Arete app has its own policy at areteprograms.com/privacy.

Who we are

Arete is run by MGM Agency, which is responsible for your data. For anything about your data, email davidtechi7@outlook.com.

What we keep

  • Your name and email address.
  • What you train for and where your sessions are recorded (Garmin, Strava or something else).
  • Which site sent you to the page, if the link said so.
  • When you joined, and that you ticked the consent box.

What we use it for

To give out the 100 test slots in the order people joined, and to email you about the test and the launch. Nothing else. We don't sell it, share it or use it for advertising. The basis is your consent, given with the tick box on the form.

Who handles it

Netlify hosts this page and stores the waiting list, on servers in the United States. Resend sends the email confirming your place; it sees your name, your email address and that email. YouTube plays the video, but only once you press play; until then nothing is loaded from YouTube.

The waiting list is kept separately from the Arete app and its data.

Cookies

This page sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking. It remembers whether you chose light or dark in your own browser only.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until Arete opens to the public, whichever comes first. If you create an Arete account before then, the app's policy covers that account.

Your rights

The email confirming your place has a link that takes you off the list and deletes your details straight away. You can also ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you at any time by emailing davidtechi7@outlook.com; we do it within 7 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
