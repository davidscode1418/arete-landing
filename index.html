Inside Arete

A coach that shows its working.

Screens from the app, filled in with a sample athlete.

01 · Every morning

The coach speaks first.

Open the app and get a call on today's session, written like a coach would say it. Under it: your fitness, fatigue and form, and how many weeks are left to race day.

02 · After every session

Every session analysed against the plan.

Power, heart rate, pace and elevation over time, how much of the plan you did, and what the session did to your fitness. An easy day that drifted too hard gets flagged.

03 · Before every session

Know why each session is there.

Every workout comes as steps with your own power, pace and heart rate targets. Tap the coach's explanation for the goal, why it's in the plan this week and how it should feel. Then send it to your watch.

04 · Every week

Fitness, fatigue and form, watched for you.

Fitness going up while form keeps falling is a warning, and the coach treats it as one. Cross the back off line and your next days get easier before you dig a hole.

05 · Under a minute

A morning check in that changes the day.

Sleep, soreness and motivation. If the signs say you're run down, today's session gets easier and the hard work moves. If they say go, you go.

06 · The whole season

Planned back from race day.

A long, patient base, then build, peak and taper, sized to the hours you actually have. Change your race, your hours or your days and the plan rebuilds from today.

Syncs with Garmin Connect and Strava. Finished sessions come in on their own, and planned workouts go to your Garmin.

About me

I'm David. I coached myself to 122nd in the world.

From 2020 to 2023 I trained and raced triathlon full time, with no coach but myself, and climbed to 122nd in the world among roughly 150,000 amateur 70.3 athletes.

Most of that came from getting the unglamorous parts right: a long, patient base, easy days kept truly easy, and backing off before fatigue turned into injury. Arete is those rules written down as a coach that applies them every single day, to your training.
